# Seattle_Library_Accessibility_map
### Description of Project: During my post-grad break, I found a love for reading books. Reading books has always been an escape for me, like going into a different world through reading a story. The convenience of my local library has given me a place to pick up books I love and decompress or get work done. Libraries are an important part of Seattle's community, where people get free resources, learn about different topics, and have a space to be in community. Something important in making sure libraries can be a pivotal space for people to grab books is this: I found that a project that focused on Seattle library accessibility can help people to look at what libraries are accessible in different neighborhoods in Seattle.
